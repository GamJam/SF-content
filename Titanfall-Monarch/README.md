# Titanfall 2 Monarch

The Monarch (Vanguard chassis) from the Garry's Mod addon "Titanfall 2 - Titans", exported for the Starfall chip
`monarch_titan.txt` (a copy is in this folder): an auto-titan that follows and fights for its owner, and that the
owner can embark and pilot. Everything is drawn and played on each player's game, so the addon isn't needed.

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `monarch.obj` | 56 hologram pieces (objects `p0`..`p55`), the model's LOD 2 with the XO-16 in its hands, bind pose |
| `monarch.txt` | skeleton (55 bones), piece bone blends and materials, attachments, 60 animations, 30 sequences |
| `monarch_anims.bin` | animated bone tracks (int16) |
| `monarch_0.png` `_2` `_3` `_8` | body, shoulder pods, cockpit and XO-16 textures, 1024 x 1024 |
| `sounds/` | mono WAV: XO-16, rockets, footsteps, dash, punch, Titanfall, embark, and Monarch's voice lines |

Made with the exporter in `Titanfall exporter` (see its README.txt). Models, textures, animations and sounds belong
to Respawn Entertainment; the addon port is by Spalishe.
