Tu-95 "Bear" for Starfall (chip: starfall\gj vehicles\tu95\tu-95_bear_glide.txt)

Model: Tupolev Tu-95 "Bear" by BARANGER Emmanuel (helijah), http://helijah.free.fr
Textures: N-SCOT. Made for FlightGear. Licensed GPL (see ORIGINAL_README.txt).

Converted for Starfall: game units (20 per meter), x forward, y left, z up, triangulated,
normals added, split into parts (propellers, control surfaces, gear legs, wheels, doors,
glass, cockpit, body). tu95_cockpit.jpg packs the cockpit instrument textures into one atlas.
